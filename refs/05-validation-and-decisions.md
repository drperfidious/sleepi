# Validation and decisions

Checked 2026-10-01. This supplement supersedes conflicting implementation advice in references 01–04. The original references are preserved. “Confirmed” describes documentation or local evidence, not an overnight device test.

## Findings that change the build

| Reference claim | Finding | Build decision / evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Series 8 stops at watchOS 26 | Confirmed by Apple's current compatibility list, which starts at Series 9 / Ultra 2 / SE 3. | Keep iOS 26 and watchOS 26 minimums. [Apple compatibility](https://www.apple.com/os/watchos/) |
| A sleep workout can alter Activity rings even without a saved workout | Confirmed by Apple DTS. | No workout APIs, workout background modes, Health writes, or write-permission requests anywhere in the app. [DTS](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/810690) |
| Sleep Smart's workout is definitely what broke Vitals | Plausible mechanism, not an established diagnosis of that app. No instrumented reproduction supplied. | Separate the verified ring effect from the unverified Vitals causal claim. Real-device comparison is required. |
| Apple has no nap tracking | **Falsified.** Apple's 2025 technical paper describes tracking outside the primary session, including possible naps, introduced with watchOS 11. | Consume Apple's records. Do not market native nap detection as absent or invent daytime sleep from stillness. [Apple technical paper, 2025](https://www.apple.com/my/healthcare/docs/site/Estimating_Sleep_Stages_from_Apple_Watch_Oct_2025.pdf) |
| Apple misses about a quarter of wake | Qualified. Apple's original median specificity was 75.0%; the updated algorithm reports 78.9%, with sensitivity changing from 97.9% to 96.8%. These are study distributions, not a correction factor for a person's night. | Show stages as estimated. Never add 21–25% “missing wake.” [Apple technical paper](https://www.apple.com/my/healthcare/docs/site/Estimating_Sleep_Stages_from_Apple_Watch_Oct_2025.pdf) |
| Wearables have difficulty distinguishing quiet wake from sleep | Confirmed directionally. The 2025 six-device study reports high sleep sensitivity and much lower wake specificity; its participants, hardware and algorithms differ from Apple's validation. | Do not pool the percentages or claim our heuristic improves accuracy. [Schyvens et al.](https://academic.oup.com/sleepadvances/article/6/2/zpaf021/8090472) |
| A hand-tuned fusion engine will “fix” Apple wake detection | **Unvalidated hypothesis.** Additional noisy signals need not improve a strong classifier. Walch's historical Watch dataset is not validation against today's Apple algorithm. | Keep a proposal engine in a device pilot. Require two contiguous motion epochs and nearby HR evidence. Suggestions never change Apple's durations. Check-ins record feedback without pretending to train a model. |
| Real-time Apple sleep stages can guide gentle wake | **Falsified as an available public API.** Stages arrive after the event, with no promised latency. | The Watch experiment reacts to movement, not “light sleep.” No continuous HR assumption. [Apple DTS](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/827920) |
| CMSensorRecorder guarantees overnight coverage | The API documents 50 Hz, up to 12 hours, and 3-day availability. A developer's sleep-time gaps are real reported evidence; DTS did not establish a definitive cause or guarantee a fix. | Empty coverage remains empty. Export a coverage-bearing 30-second summary; raw accelerometer data stays on Watch. Do not use a workout fallback. [Recorder](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/coremotion/cmsensorrecorder/recordaccelerometer(forduration:)), [DTS](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/827920) |
| A smart-alarm runtime lasts 30 minutes and can be scheduled 36 hours ahead | Confirmed, with crucial constraints: schedule while the Watch app is active, attach the delegate on relaunch, only one scheduled session, and notify before expiry. | Confirm time on Watch. Use a 25-minute window with expiry margin, a deadline fallback, and visible failure state. `WKBackgroundModes` is `alarm`; there is no invented `smart-alarm` plist setting. [Runtime sessions](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/watchkit/using-extended-runtime-sessions), [background modes](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/bundleresources/information-property-list/wkbackgroundmodes) |
| Gentle wake can reliably ring at the selected latest time | Not guaranteed: power, delivery, lifecycle and runtime invalidation remain relevant. | It is explicitly an optional nudge. Keep Apple's Clock alarm; do not label the nudge an alarm replacement. A haptic repeats until dismissed, rather than an undocumented one-off tap. [notifyUser](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/watchkit/wkextendedruntimesession/notifyuser(haptictype:repeathandler:)) |
| Nightly HealthKit stages support a valid SRI | **Not established.** SRI compares sleep/wake states 24 hours apart. Missing samples do not establish wakefulness, and nightly stages are not complete 24-hour observation. | Ship circular clock-time variation over 7–14 recorded nights. Label it schedule consistency, not SRI. This is a methodological inference from SRI's input requirements. [Primary SRI validation](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-018-32402-5) |
| Sleep debt / recovery score are validated calculations | The proposed target shortfall and weighted vitals score were not validated by the refs. | Expose a named target shortfall with coverage; do not infer biological debt, subtract recovery credit, or generate a recovery/readiness score. Show raw vitals and nightly-median baselines. |
| New apps can affect Health source priority | Confirmed for writing sources. | Remove **all** correction writes, including the contradictory hidden Labs writer and any “ship on by default” escape hatch. No speculative gap fills: Apple's late-arriving records could subsequently overlap them. [Health sources](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108779) |
| sleepi appears in Health only if it writes | **False as a blanket acceptance criterion.** Authorization can make an app appear in Sources even without permission to share. Source listing and contribution of samples are different. | Verify no contributed samples / no write authorization; don't fail a read-only listing. [Health authorization](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/healthkit/authorizing-access-to-health-data) |
| Empty Health results reveal denied permissions | **False.** HealthKit intentionally does not disclose read-denial status. | Use “unavailable or access may be off.” Never show a fabricated “authorized” state from `authorizationStatus(for:)`, which concerns sharing. [Health authorization](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/healthkit/authorizing-access-to-health-data) |
| Sleep setup and Vitals prerequisites can be checked programmatically | Not with the APIs in scope. Apple's Vitals setup explicitly needs Track Sleep, Sleep Focus, and a baseline. | Present a user checklist, not green checks inferred by the app. Do not treat Focus time as sleep onset. [Vitals requirements](https://support.apple.com/en-us/120142), [sleep setup](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108906) |
| Wrist temperature is an absolute read-only value available from night one | Confirmed. Health's displayed baseline deviation is a separate representation. | Label absolute °C and our own prior-night median clearly. [Wrist temperature](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/healthkit/hkquantitytypeidentifier/applesleepingwristtemperature) |
| Breathing disturbances / apnea notifications should be expected on Series 8 | Series 8 is not in Apple's apnea-notification hardware list. Do not assume that requesting a quantity implies available data. | Read opportunistically; missing stays unavailable. SDK header `HKTypeIdentifiers.h` specifies **count**, not count/hour. Guard unit compatibility before converting. [Apple support](https://support.apple.com/en-us/120031) |
| An Apple Sleep Score HealthKit type is available | No public type was found in the examined documentation and local SDK. Absence is bounded to those sources. | Do not display a score impersonating Apple's. [Sleep Score API discussion](https://developer.apple.com/forums/thread/800403) |
| Health background delivery guarantees a morning notification | **Overstated.** Delivery is best effort and data may arrive piecemeal. | Observe all requested types; query a bounded fresh snapshot; refresh on foreground; deduplicate notifications. Notifications say only that a summary is ready. [Background delivery](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/healthkit/hkhealthstore/enablebackgrounddelivery(for:frequency:withcompletion:)) |
| Short audio highlights can report calibrated room-noise dB | **Unsupported.** Digital amplitude without calibration is dBFS, not dB SPL. Clip duration is not snore duration. | Label dBFS, avoid snore-minute / full-night average claims, and preserve classifier uncertainty and attribution uncertainty. A “not me” label is a manual annotation, not speaker recognition. |
| Built-in snoring/speech/cough labels exist | Confirmed on this Mac by constructing `SNClassifySoundRequest(.version1)` and enumerating `knownClassifications`. iPhone availability still needs checking. | Intersect a label allowlist at runtime; fail visibly if no supported labels exist. No audio uploads. [SoundAnalysis](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/soundanalysis/snclassifysoundrequest) |
| Complete file protection supports writing new clips while locked | Contradictory requirements without additional handling. | Use protection until first user authentication for the explicit overnight feature, and exclude the entire local library from backup. Document the tradeoff instead of claiming full locked-file inaccessibility. |
| Notes and tags are necessarily non-health data safe to sync | **Unsafe assumption.** Sleep journals and substance/habit tags can reveal personal health information. | All settings, notes, derived observations and audio remain local. No CloudKit entitlement. [App Review 5.1.3](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/) |
| A Live Activity guarantees recording consent compliance or lasts all night | Neither is established. It is not the source of recording truth and has lifecycle limits. | Explicit foreground consent, system microphone indicator and actual engine status; the activity says recording was requested and links back to check. Interruptions stop capture and need a fresh start. |
| A custom-scheme `OpenURLIntent` is appropriate for controls | Incorrect API choice: it is documented for universal links. | Use a shared `OpenIntent` in app and extension; retain a cold-launch route. Regular widgets may use `widgetURL` with the app scheme. [OpenURLIntent](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appintents/openurlintent), [controls](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/widgetkit/creating-controls-to-perform-actions-across-the-system) |

## What was not independently established

The market table's exact prices, complaint prevalence, app ranking claims, Sleep Smart identity, and competitor implementation details are not adopted as engineering facts. Pillow's published automatic-mode limitation is corroborated by its [own page](https://pillow.app/automatic-sleep-tracking-for-the-apple-watch), but that page's age makes it inadequate to certify every current version. No purchase decision depends on those prices.

The exact Robbins deep-sleep estimate was not revalidated here (the publisher returned a rate limit). Likewise, none of the coaching interventions, smart-alarm outcome claims, snore diagnostic performance, or medical risk scores were implemented. sleepi makes no clinical efficacy claims. The build does not imply that every cited research number has been independently reproduced.

“Do no harm” cannot be established by unit tests or by demanding identical physiology on different nights. Zero Health writes is enforceable in code; preservation of Apple's tracking, alarms, Vitals, runtime behavior and battery needs controlled testing without a debugger attached.
